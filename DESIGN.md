# Mundial Predictions Design System

## Theme & Aesthetic
- High-end, premium sports aesthetic suitable for a global event like the World Cup.
- Uses glassmorphism (translucent backgrounds with blur effects) for modern overlay panels.
- Vibrant, energetic colors associated with football: Deep Pitch Green, Championship Gold, and sleek Dark Navy/Carbon Black for text and contrast.
- Subte micro-animations on hover and active states (like glowing borders or subtle scaling).
- Typography should be bold, readable, and athletic-feeling (e.g., Roboto, Inter, or Outfit).

## Colors
- Primary: #15803d (Pitch Green)
- Secondary: #eab308 (Championship Gold)
- Background: Very light grayish-green or a subtle stadium pattern/gradient (#f0fdf4 to #f8fafc).
- Accents: #dc2626 (Red for live matches or alerts), #2563eb (Blue for standard links).

## Components
- Cards: White or slightly translucent, heavy shadow, rounded corners (xl or 2xl).
- Inputs: Large, highly visible, with green or gold focus rings.
- Buttons: Gradients (Green to Dark Green, or Gold to Orange), bold text, distinct hover effects.
- Badges/Pills: For match status ("EN JUEGO" in red pulse, "PREVIA" in blue/gray).

## Layout
- Mobile-first approach, max-width centered layout for the prediction forms.
- Sticky header with high contrast.
